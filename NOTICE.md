# Assets and dependency review

The owner supplied the immutable PNG design references. Their redistribution rights have not been independently verified. They remain design inputs; no reference PNG is packaged as an application screen. The native vector/canvas artwork is a derivative implementation candidate.

Dependency versions are recorded in `gradle/libs.versions.toml`. No tunnel library, provider SDK, telemetry SDK, network client or proprietary font is included in this launch-only stage. Gradle-generated wrapper scripts/JAR retain their upstream notices.

Full resolved dependency license inventory, security-advisory review, asset-rights confirmation, signing and store-policy review remain release gates. This development branch makes no licensing-audit, security-audit or production-readiness claim.

## Provenance of the shipped brand artwork (merged TASK_00)

- `assets/reference/*.png` are owner-supplied design references. They are **not**
  packaged into the APK and none is rendered as a screen. Verify with:
  `python3 tools/verify_apk_asset_hygiene.py` (exit 0). That script checks the exact
  11 asset basenames, rejects any PNG over 900 KB so a renamed reference asset still
  trips it, confirms no font file is bundled, and scans text/XML/pb/assets entries for
  private or preshared key material. It is also a CI step.
- The in-app emblem is drawn natively in Compose Canvas by
  `app/src/main/kotlin/com/kx/vpn/ui/brand/KXOrb.kt`. The launcher and system-splash
  mark is generated by `tools/gen_brand_vector.py` from the same normalised geometry.
  Both are derivative works of the supplied artwork.
- Colour tokens were pixel-sampled from `00-brand-logo.png` with System.Drawing probes;
  the probe coordinates and the deltas against `docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` are recorded in
  `docs/evidence/TASK_00.md`.
- Typography uses the platform sans-serif family. No proprietary or downloaded font is
  bundled, which is also what keeps Arabic shaping on the device's own Arabic fallback.
- Third-party runtime libraries are AndroidX, Kotlin stdlib and kotlinx.coroutines, all
  Apache-2.0. Test-only additions are JUnit 4 (EPL), Robolectric (MIT) and its
  `android-all` artifacts (Apache-2.0 via the AOSP source they repackage).
